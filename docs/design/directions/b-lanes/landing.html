Pay your business bills without publishing your business.

Obliq is accounts payable for crypto-native finance teams. Capture an invoice, approve it under policy, settle it in shielded Zcash from a signer you operate, and reconcile the payment back to the bill.

Obliq never holds a spending key. It can record an approval. It cannot sign a payment.
