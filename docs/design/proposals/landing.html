Obliq can record an approval. It cannot sign a payment.

Accounts payable for teams that settle in shielded Zcash. Capture a bill, apply policy and approvals, hand an exact payment request to a signer you operate, then reconcile what the network shows.

Public-network settlement is blocked until Obliq proves public synchronization and a funded shielded payment.

Three authorities, kept in separate hands

A payment crosses from one lane to the next only by a deliberate hand-off. No lane can act for another.

Business approval Your finance team, recorded in Obliq
  1. Capture the bill
  2. Evaluate policy
  3. Approve
  4. Mark ready to settle
Signature A signer you operate, outside Obliq
  1. Inspect the exact request
  2. Sign
  3. Broadcast
Observation Read-only observer with a viewing key
  1. Detect the output
  2. Count confirmations
  3. Mark settled
  • No spending key on the server.

    Obliq accepts no seed, PCZT, raw transaction or signer credential.

  • Approval is not a signature.

    An approval records business intent. Only your signer can authorize the transaction.

  • The viewing key is still sensitive.

    The observer cannot spend, but a leaked viewing key reveals account activity.
