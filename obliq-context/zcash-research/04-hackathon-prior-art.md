# Recent Zcash Hackathon Prior Art

## ZecHub Hackathon 3.0

The 2026 event reported 36 submissions across: - Infrastructure -
Games - FROST - Zcash Login - Accounting

Judging emphasized: - working functionality - meaningful Zcash usage -
completeness - originality - ecosystem impact - documentation and
testability

Notable shapes included: - ZEC-OS infrastructure/explorer tooling -
ZcashMe private authentication - collaborative custody/FROST ideas -
accounting/financial-management prototypes - games

Lesson: The bar is above merely calling a Zcash API. The product should
use privacy or another Zcash primitive in a way central to the user's
value.

## Current community hackathon briefs

Recent Zcash community planning identifies still-useful areas such as: -
shielded point of sale - shielded payouts - browser/Snap-native
applications - Ironwood tooling - SDK hardening

These are useful ecosystem needs, but Colosseum is startup-oriented. A
developer tool or one-off reference implementation needs a business
wedge if we choose it.

## Startup lesson for Colosseum

A good submission should answer: 1. Who has the painful problem? 2. Why
is privacy essential rather than decorative? 3. Why does Zcash give us
an advantage? 4. What is the repeated workflow? 5. Who pays? 6. Why can
this become a company? 7. What working evidence proves the hardest part?
