# Startup Selection Rubric

Score each candidate from 1 to 5.

## 1. Pain

Does a specific user experience the problem repeatedly and urgently?

## 2. Zcash necessity

Would the product become materially worse on a transparent chain?

A score of 5 means privacy/selective disclosure is central to the
product.

## 3. Product depth

Can this become a system people return to rather than a one-shot tool?

## 4. Business model

Is there an identifiable buyer and credible way to charge?

## 5. Market expansion

Can the initial wedge expand into adjacent workflows?

## 6. Technical defensibility

Does the team learn/build something difficult and reusable rather than
only a frontend?

## 7. Demonstrability

Can judges see the core value in a short demo with real Zcash behavior?

## 8. Ecosystem differentiation

Is the wedge meaningfully different from current Zcash wallets,
processors, hackathon projects and grants?

## 9. Security realism

Can the architecture avoid reckless custody, key handling and privacy
claims?

## 10. Distribution

Is there a plausible way to reach the first 10, 100 and 1,000 users?

Reject ideas with: - privacy added only as marketing - no repeated
workflow - no buyer - no reason for Zcash - a demo that could be
replaced by a mocked transaction - a feature already provided well by
Zodl or an active Zcash project
